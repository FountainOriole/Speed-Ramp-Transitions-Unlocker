## ⚡ What is This?

**Speed Ramp Transitions** is a desktop toolkit for video editors who want professional motion graphics without the steep learning curve. It provides a curated library of **speed ramping curves, transition presets, and motion templates** that you can apply to your footage in seconds.

Whether you're editing cinematic montages, gaming highlights, or social media reels — this toolkit gives you the smooth, punchy motion that makes edits feel professional.
